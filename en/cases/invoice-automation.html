Featured case · Automation + BI

XML invoices to operational control.

Google Workspace flow to capture invoices from email, structure XML data, manage status in AppSheet and publish indicators in Looker Studio.

View dashboard Repository

Impact

Time per invoice 90 -> 25 min
Estimated savings ~60 h/month
Traceability 100%

Problem

  • Invoices arrived as ZIP files with XML and PDF documents.
  • The team had to download, open, validate, reply and archive manually.
  • Traceability depended on folders and later searches.
  • Cost control was not consolidated in an analytical source.

Solution

Gmail labels invoice emails Apps Script downloads ZIP files, extracts XML/PDF and saves evidence in Drive Google Sheets consolidates fields, links, status and traceability AppSheet lets the team manage acceptance, rejection or redirection Looker Studio publishes spend, status, suppliers and budget indicators
